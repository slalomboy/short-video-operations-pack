# Limitations and public boundary

[中文主入口](../../README.md) · [English docs](README.md)

- No platform login or automatic publishing.
- No permission to spend money or start a live stream.
- No guarantee of views, audience growth, leads, conversion, or return on ad spend.
- Platform rules and third-party terms must be checked at the time of use.
- Public examples are anonymous and do not contain source videos, transcripts, credentials, account data, or customer information.
- Paid-growth and live-conversion Skills provide bounded decision records only; real cost, fulfillment, and compliance evidence remain external inputs.
