This page lists the independently useful outcomes in fixed source version `v0.1.0-alpha.5`. Stable IDs provide traceability; they do not change the thirteen-Skill suite structure.

| ID | Finished outcome | Minimum input → output | Free handout | Suite upgrade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FIN-SV-000` | Thirteen-Skill operating chain from positioning to experiments and review | Business goal, account, audience, and available material → `ShortVideoOpsJob` plus stage results | Thirteen-Skill operating map | PA complete workflow, templates, cases, and state gates |
| `FIN-SV-001` | Account positioning card | Offer, audience, and business goal → promise, content pillars, and exclusions | One-page positioning card | PA audience, topics, publishing, and review |
| `FIN-SV-002` | Audience jobs-and-objections card | Interviews, comments, or queries → situations, jobs, concerns, and decision criteria | Audience evidence worksheet | PA sample library and topic connection |
| `FIN-SV-003` | Prioritized, filmable topic list | Positioning, audience, goal, and evidence → six-dimension scorecard | Topic scorecard | PA trend calibration and content batches |
| `FIN-SV-006` | Evidence and pre-publication revision list | Script, shot plan, or edit facts → blockers, evidence gaps, and actions | Twenty-point review checklist | PA/PB evidence and rights records |
| `FIN-SV-007` | Executable shooting and shot plan | Approved script, format, and assets → shots, screen recordings, proof visuals, and asset tasks | One-page shot plan | PB VideoJob and final acceptance |
| `FIN-SV-010` | Searchable material ledger with rights boundaries | Files or links, provenance, and rights → cards, index, and reuse status | Material provenance register | PA/PB asset library and reuse history |
| `FIN-SV-011` | Publishing experiment and performance conclusion | Reviewed content, hypothesis, and real data → experiment record, review, and next test | Single-video experiment sheet | PA batch experiments and periodic review |

Each item may be invoked directly, but all inherit the parent repository version. Paid-growth and live-conversion entries remain bounded suite components rather than separately validated finished products.

## Verifiable boundary

- Structure, routing, job contracts, and deterministic tests are verified.
- Audience or demand statements remain hypotheses when no user sample exists.
- The repository does not sign in, upload, publish, authorize spending, or guarantee reach, conversion, or revenue.
- `FIN-SV-012` is a public add-on outside the thirteen-Skill core; upload, submission, scheduling, and public readback remain separate states.
- A fixed source Tag is not a GitHub Release.
